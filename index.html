OSR MAP MAKER

Interactive map canvas (requires a mouse or touch).
Select/Move drags symbols, names, labels and rivers/roads/trails/borders (selected = gold); double-click (or Enter) edits a name/label/river name, RHVI transform, G GM-only, Del removes, arrows nudge, Ctrl+C/V/D copy/paste/duplicate. Drag empty map to pan. Draw River/Road/Trail are freehand and smoothed as you draw; a river's end snaps onto another river (gold ring), starting on a river's end extends it, and it widens toward water. Hex Border snaps to hex edges. Fill floods a patch of terrain; Room drags out a floor rectangle (Shift = rock); with Snap to grid doors snap into walls. ¼-inch squares, export up to 300 dpi. Esc cancels & deselects, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+S save .json. Pan hold Space, middle-drag, wheel or arrows. Zoom +− or Ctrl+wheel / pinch, 0 resets, Fit sizes to window. On touch, drag = draw, two fingers = pan & zoom (a stray first-finger mark is undone).
