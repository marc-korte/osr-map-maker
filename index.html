A quick field guide

Explore the map

Choose Pan to move without editing, or hold Space and drag. Use two fingers to pan and pinch on touch screens. Fit shows the whole map. + / − zoom; 0 resets. A mouse wheel pans; Ctrl + wheel zooms.

Build your map

Use Edit for terrain, rooms, symbols and lines. Drag to paint. Room draws a rectangle; Shift-drag carves rock. Select/Move selects an object; double-click or Enter edits its text. Map holds dimensions, scale, layers and saved snapshots.

Shape and revise

Ctrl+Z undoes; Ctrl+Y redoes. R rotates, H / V flip, I inverts. Arrows nudge a selection; Del removes it. Ctrl+C / V / D copy, paste and duplicate. On a Mac, use ⌘ instead of Ctrl.

Prepare for play

Generate creates a map you can keep editing. Use GM key for private notes and a printable Markdown reference. G hides a selection from Player view. PNG uses the current GM/Player view. Project → Save backup includes all secrets and notes; Ctrl+S saves a backup.
